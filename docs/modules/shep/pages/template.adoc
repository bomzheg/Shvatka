== Summary

Two or three sentences: what changes, and what the reader gets out of it. Enough
for someone who will never read the rest.

== Context

What exists today, what hurts about it, what forced the question. Facts and
constraints, not the solution — code references (`shvatka/core/...`) beat prose.

== Decision

What we are going to do, and why this option and not the neighbouring one. Split
into as many sections as the change has moving parts: data model, domain model,
API surface, transactions, delivery channels. Each section states the decision
first and the reasoning after it.

Alternatives that were seriously considered belong here too, with the reason
they lost. A rejected option nobody wrote down comes back next quarter.

== Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, what it costs. Migrations, one-way
doors, performance, extra surface to maintain.

== Phases and status

The plan broken into landable steps, each carrying its own state. Update this
section as the phases land — this is what makes the SHEP worth reading a year
later.

== Deviations from the plan

Filled in during implementation: everywhere the code ended up different from
what this document proposed, and whether that was deliberate.

== Open questions

`Decision` when it is settled, with a note of what was decided.
